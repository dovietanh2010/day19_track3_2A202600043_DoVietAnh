OpenAI was founded in 2015 by Sam Altman, Elon Musk, Greg Brockman, Ilya Sutskever, Wojciech Zaremba, and John Schulman.
In 2019, Microsoft invested $1 billion in OpenAI to support the development of artificial general intelligence.
Sam Altman is the CEO of OpenAI, while Greg Brockman serves as the President.
ChatGPT, a large language model developed by OpenAI, was released in November 2022.
Microsoft expanded its partnership with OpenAI in 2023 with a multi-year, multi-billion dollar investment.
Google launched Gemini, its own advanced AI model, in 2023 to compete with OpenAI's GPT-4.
DeepMind, a subsidiary of Alphabet (Google's parent company), was founded by Demis Hassabis, Shane Legg, and Mustafa Suleyman in 2010.
Alphabet acquired DeepMind in 2014 for approximately $500 million.
Sundar Pichai is the CEO of both Alphabet and Google.
NVIDIA, led by CEO Jensen Huang, provides the H100 GPUs that power most modern LLM training.
Anthropic was founded in 2021 by former OpenAI executives Daniela Amodei and Dario Amodei.
Amazon invested $4 billion in Anthropic in 2023 to secure a stake in the generative AI market.
Meta released Llama, an open-source large language model, in 2023.
Mark Zuckerberg is the founder and CEO of Meta.
In 2024, Microsoft announced the formation of Microsoft AI, a new organization led by Mustafa Suleyman.
Satya Nadella has been the CEO of Microsoft since 2014.
Apple announced Apple Intelligence in 2024, integrating AI across its device ecosystem.
Tim Cook is the CEO of Apple.
Mistral AI, a French AI startup, was founded in 2023 by former researchers from Meta and Google DeepMind.
Tesla, led by Elon Musk, is developing Grok through its xAI subsidiary.
